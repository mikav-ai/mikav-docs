# Introduction

Welcome to the Mikav documentation.

**Mikav** is an open-source AI copilot and open Malayalam model powering Kerala's creative and cultural ecosystem — built for everyone to use, and for businesses to build on.

## What is Mikav?

Mikav is a family of AI models and tools designed to understand and generate Malayalam with deep cultural awareness. Whether you're a developer building the next great app, a content creator looking for intelligent assistance, or a business seeking to serve Malayalam-speaking users — Mikav is built for you.

## Key Features

- **Open Malayalam Model** — A language model trained on diverse Malayalam text, understanding idioms, context, and culture.
- **AI Copilot** — Intelligent assistance for writing, coding, translation, and creative work.
- **Open Source** — Transparent, auditable, and community-driven development.
- **Enterprise Ready** — APIs and SDKs for businesses to integrate Mikav into their products.

## Who is this for?

| Audience | Use Case |
|----------|----------|

| Creators | Get intelligent writing and creative assistance |
