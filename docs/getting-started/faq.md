# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### What is Mikav?

Mikav is an open-source AI copilot and open Malayalam model built for Kerala's creative and cultural ecosystem. It provides language models, APIs, and tools designed to understand and generate Malayalam with cultural context.

### Is Mikav free to use?

Yes, Mikav is open-source and free for individuals, creators, and communities. We also offer enterprise plans for businesses that need higher rate limits, dedicated support, and SLAs.

### What languages does Mikav support?

Mikav is primarily focused on Malayalam, but our models also support English and code generation. Multi-language support is on our roadmap.

## Technical

### What models are available?

See our [Models Overview](../models/overview.md) for a full list of available models and their capabilities.

### How do I get an API key?

Sign up at [mikav.in](https://mikav.in) and navigate to your dashboard to generate an API key.

### What are the rate limits?

Rate limits depend on your plan. Free tier users get a generous allocation for personal use. See our pricing page for details.

### Can I fine-tune Mikav models?

Yes. We provide fine-tuning capabilities for enterprise users. Check our [Models documentation](../models/how-to-use.md) for details.
